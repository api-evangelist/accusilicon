# Accusilicon

> Accusilicon (Guangzhou Ruixin Microelectronics Co., Ltd. / 广州睿芯微电子有限公司) is a fabless
> mixed-signal semiconductor company in Guangzhou, China. It designs ultra-low phase noise
> XO/VCXO/TCXO and OCXO oscillator ICs, clock ICs and modules, RF/analog switch ICs,
> mobile-interface protection ICs, ADC/DAC converters, digital audio processor (DSP) and audio
> ICs, and spectral image sensor chips, plus clock stability analyzers and custom ASIC/SoC,
> module, system and clock design services.
